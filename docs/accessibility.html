1. General

We place great importance on making CookSync accessible to people with disabilities and work to align it with the Israeli Standard SI 5568, based on WCAG 2.0 Level AA.

2. Accessibility adjustments

The app follows your phone's own accessibility settings rather than adding a separate menu: all text scales with the system Font size and Display size settings, and the app works with TalkBack, High contrast text, and Color correction. It uses standard Android interface components and keeps cooking-mode screens readable at a glance with large text and high-contrast colors.

This documentation site uses semantic HTML with a logical heading structure and full keyboard operability. The accessibility button at the bottom-right of every page lets you enlarge text (up to 150%), turn on high contrast, and underline links; your choice is remembered in your browser.

3. Known limitations

CookSync is under continuous improvement. If you encounter an accessibility barrier in the app or on this site, please report it - we treat these reports as bugs to fix.

4. Contact for accessibility requests

For accessibility questions, requests, or reports, contact yaronserlindev@gmail.com. We are committed to responding within 14 days.
